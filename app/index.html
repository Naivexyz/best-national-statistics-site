Japan in Numbers is a demonstration statistics portal. Figures are the publicly known magnitudes published by the sources named beside each one — the World Bank, the Statistics Bureau of Japan, the OECD and Japan's ministries — rounded for readability. Treat them as an accurate portrait, not an official release: for citation, follow the source and year to the publisher's own table.

Static site: every chart and table is drawn from the data files shipped with this page. No network, no tracking.
